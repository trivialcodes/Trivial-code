Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At Trivial Code, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our clients, their users, and visitors to our website. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you engage with our services, including custom websites, applications, and interactive study guides.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to provide our services efficiently:

  • Client Information: Name, email address, phone number, and billing details required for communication and monthly retainer subscriptions.
  • Project Data: Assets, text, intellectual property, and proprietary code required to build your digital products.
  • End-User Data (For Apps & Guides): If we build an application or interactive study guide for you, we may process end-user data (e.g., student passcodes, usage logs) strictly on your behalf as a data processor.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for business operations, including:

  • Designing, developing, and deploying custom websites and applications.
  • Managing access control for interactive study guides via temporary passcode systems.
  • Providing ongoing maintenance, security updates, and customer support.
  • Processing monthly retainer payments.

3. Code & Intellectual Property Security

Your digital products are built on modern, secure infrastructure. We utilize third-party cloud services to store and host your projects:

  • Code Storage: All source code is hosted securely in private repositories on GitHub. We do not store client source code permanently on local devices.
  • Hosting & Databases: We utilize trusted cloud providers (such as Vercel and Supabase) to deploy your websites and manage databases. These providers comply with strict international security standards.
  • Interactive Study Guides: To protect your intellectual property from unauthorized distribution, our study guides utilize a centralized database architecture. Access is granted via temporary passcodes and automatically revoked upon the agreed-upon expiration date. End-users never download or possess the source code.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share data with trusted infrastructure partners (e.g., cloud hosts, payment processors) necessary to deliver our services. All third-party partners are bound by confidentiality and stringent data protection agreements.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information and project data for as long as your monthly retainer or subscription is active. Upon cancellation of services, client websites and apps will be taken offline, and proprietary data may be deleted from our active servers in accordance with our terms of service.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate data, or request the deletion of your data, subject to legal and contractual obligations.
